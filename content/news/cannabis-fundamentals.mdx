Cannabis can feel complex when you are new. Fundamentals make the experience clearer.

Start with product type, potency, and setting. Flower, edibles, vaporizers, and tinctures each offer different onset and duration. Individual experiences vary, so go slowly and stay curious.

At Legacy on Lark, education is part of hospitality. Ask questions, learn the language of terpenes and effects, and choose what fits your preferences—not what a trend dictates.

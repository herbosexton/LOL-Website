Terpenes are aromatic compounds found in many plants, including cannabis. They contribute to scent and the overall sensory profile of a cultivar or product.

You might notice citrus, pine, spice, floral, or herbal notes. Those cues can help you build preference over time. They are not guarantees of how you will feel.

Use terpenes as a vocabulary for discovery. Pair what you smell with what you enjoy, and let your own experience guide the next choice.

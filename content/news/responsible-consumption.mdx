Responsible consumption begins with legality and age: adults 21 and older only, with valid ID.

From there, intention matters. Know your setting, start low when trying something new, and never drive impaired. Store products safely away from children and pets.

Legacy is built on trust and community. Enjoy thoughtfully, respect your neighbors, and treat cannabis as one part of a fuller culture of care.
